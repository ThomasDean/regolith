Regolith privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Regolith is a small, non-commercial browser game made by Thomas Dean for playing with friends. This page explains what it keeps about you.

If you just play on this device

Games save in your browser's own storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.

If you sign in or play online

  • Google sign-in: Regolith receives your Google account's name, email address and profile picture link, plus an ID that identifies the account. It doesn't receive your password or anything else from your Google account.
  • Guest play: you get an anonymous ID tied to this browser, with no name or email.
  • Game data: your cloud saves, the online games you're in, and the name you choose for your seat.
  • Turn alerts (optional): if you turn them on, your device's push address and keys are stored so Regolith can tell you when it's your move. You can turn them off in the Play online panel, which deletes them.

This is stored with Supabase, which hosts Regolith's database and sign-in. It's used only to run the game: signing you in, syncing your saves and showing your seat to the other players in your games. It isn't sold, shared for advertising, or used for anything else. Regolith has no ads or analytics trackers.

Deleting your data

Delete individual cloud saves from the Cloud saves panel. To remove your account and everything tied to it, email thomas@deanhome.net from the address you sign in with.
